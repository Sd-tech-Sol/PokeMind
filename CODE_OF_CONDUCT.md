# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

PokeMind is an open project about a game people play against other people. Its data — battle logs, decks,
accounts — names other players. We therefore expect particular care from everyone who takes part, both in
respecting other people and in respecting their data.

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, sexual orientation, or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Showing empathy, patience and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, play styles and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Putting the interests of the project and its users first.
- Writing in English or French, as you prefer, without demanding either language of the person you are
  talking to.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks directed at a person.
- Harassment, public or private, including of players met in game.
- Publishing other people's private information — in-game name, account, address, screenshots of a
  profile — without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## A rule specific to this project: other players' data

PokeMind reads battle logs that contain the names of real opponents. As a consequence:

- **Never** attach a real battle log, a player name, an account screenshot or a personal path to an issue,
  a contribution, a message or a screenshot — not even your own.
- Use invented data only, as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- A contribution or report containing real personal data will be edited or declined. That is not a
  judgement of the person who submitted it.

This rule protects other players and contributors alike.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space — repository, issues, pull requests, discussions — and whenever
someone represents the project in a public space.

## Enforcement

The project is currently maintained by one person. Reports can be sent to the maintainer through the GitHub
profile linked in [README.md](README.md). There is no dedicated reporting address today; that limit is
acknowledged and will be revisited if the project takes on more people.

The maintainer commits to reviewing every report, to respecting the privacy and safety of the reporter, and
to responding proportionately: a warning, removal of content, declining a contribution, or, as a last
resort, exclusion from the project's spaces.

Security vulnerabilities are not covered by this document: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
