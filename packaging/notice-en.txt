PokeMind - your Pokémon TCG Live companion

- Your battles, decks and conversations stay on this PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PokeMind.
  Uninstalling PokeMind removes the program and keeps that folder.
- On first launch, PokeMind can download the public card catalogue (TCGdex, French and English,
  about 25 MB). Nothing personal is sent.
- The coach uses an engine you already have: Codex CLI, Claude Code (online, your own account) or
  Ollama (local). Nothing goes to an online engine until you allow it in Settings.
- Fair play: for practice and for reviewing games afterwards. Do not use it during official
  tournaments. PokeMind never talks to the game client: no overlay, no bot, no automation.
